verifier checks, the `--chronology` research mode, and the `/development`, dossier and methodology wording. **Not applied to production.** Applying

## The detector correction

`MULTI_ORG_SEASON_UNVERIFIED` no longer fires for a season with exactly two organizations, every stint game-dated, and exactly one canonical
Dodgers transaction of the player inside the bracket, read in the player's direction (`asset_side`): a trade between the two clubs, or the Dodgers
end of the move (a release, free agency or waiver without a counterparty before the other club's first game; a free-agent signing after its last).
A team change without a transaction, a move between two other clubs and two bracketing transactions still fire. Mirrored by
`lib/research/development-chronology.js`; `tests/db/research-053.test.mjs` proves the view and the pure rule flag the same players.

## Order

1. Back up (`pg_dump`, restore-verified on PostgreSQL 17, the through-052 verifier 255/255 on the restored copy).
2. Apply `053_transaction_chronology_resolution.sql` through the safe runner (SHA-checked). One transaction; about 12 s on PGlite, most of it the
   snapshot refresh.
3. Verify: live verifier 255/255, drift 0, generation 9 on all eleven snapshots, the counts above.
4. Merge the PR (normal merge commit); one Vercel production deploy. The application reads only relations already in the product contract
   (`v_player_development_stints`, `v_player_transactions`) and works against the pre-053 database too.
5. Smoke: `/development` (108 gaps; the transaction-blocked note), `/players/pedro-baez` (2022: the Dodgers signing between the two clubs),
   `/players/edgar-leon` (2026: the Dodgers release), `/players/carlos-rincon` and `/players/carlos-santana` (the recorded trades),
   `/players/diego-cartaya` (2025: a move between two other clubs, no transaction), `/players/jose-vizcaino` (the 2000 trade), `/methodology`,
   `/prediction-readiness` (unchanged).

Do not re-apply 053 in production to test idempotency (proven on PGlite and PostgreSQL 17). If step 2 fails nothing is committed.

## Rollback

`rollback-053.sql` (one transaction, guarded: it refuses unless every 053 event is present exactly as 053 wrote it, and refuses when later research
depends on one of its assets) deletes the 14 assets and 10 events and restores the 052 view text byte for byte. Every canonical table returns to its
exact pre-053 content; the snapshots are refreshed once (a new generation with the pre-053 content). **053 can be applied again after its rollback**
and reproduces the same content.
