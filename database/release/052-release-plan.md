## The detector correction

`v_dodgers_development_research_queue` is the Migration 024 view with two conditions changed and every other detector byte-identical:

- `SEASON_GAP` no longer fires when the only missing seasons are 2020 for an affiliated minor leaguer (his last recorded season before it is
  affiliated minor-league play only and he has no major-league stint by then). The official schedule holds no 2020 regular-season game at any
  affiliated minor-league level; the 2020 major-league season was played, so a major leaguer's missing 2020 still fires.
- `MULTI_ORG_SEASON_UNVERIFIED` no longer fires for a season with exactly two organizations, every stint game-dated, and one canonical
  transaction of the player between them dated on or after his last appearance for the first and on or before his first for the second. A team
  change without a transaction still fires.

Both rules are mirrored by `lib/research/development-chronology.js`; `tests/db/research-052.test.mjs` proves the view and the pure rules flag the
same players.

## Order

1. Back up (`pg_dump`, restore-verified on PostgreSQL 17, the through-051 verifier 252/252 on the restored copy).
2. Apply `052_development_taxonomy_chronology_resolution.sql` through the safe runner (SHA-checked). One transaction; about 10 s on PGlite, most of
   it the closing snapshot-drift proof.
3. Verify: live verifier 252/252, drift 0, generation still 8, the counts above.
4. Merge the PR (normal merge commit); one Vercel production deploy. The application reads only relations already in the product contract
   (`v_player_development_stints`, `v_player_transactions`) and works against the pre-052 database too.
5. Smoke (authenticated): `/development` (the "Why each gap is open" table; 110 gaps, no "Fact missing" column), a dossier with a bracketed
   season (`/players/carlos-rincon`), one without a transaction (`/players/diego-cartaya`), `/players/carlos-santana` (highest level at a Dodgers
   affiliate High-A), `/prediction-readiness` (unchanged).

Do not re-apply 052 in production to test idempotency (proven on PGlite and PostgreSQL 17). If step 2 fails nothing is committed.

## Rollback

`rollback-052.sql` (one transaction, guarded: it refuses unless every 052 row is in its 052 state, and a pending review someone has decided since
blocks it) restores the pre-052 organization, name, basis and citations of the 322 organization stints, NULL dates on the 528 dated stints, the
season precision, note and basis of the 101 upgraded level debuts, removes the 11 dated professional debuts, the 5 pending reviews, the three
Pioneer League era-map rows and the 052 sources, and restores the Migration 024 view text byte for byte. Every table returns to its pre-052 content
exactly; drift is proven 0 again, so no refresh runs. **052 can be applied again after its rollback** and reproduces the same content.
